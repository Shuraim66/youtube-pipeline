**Key structural finding:** genuinely Shorts-FIRST business/wealth channels are mostly unnamed AI-automation accounts (not individually documented, unverifiable subs). That anonymous, forgettable mass IS the exploitable gap — a Shorts-native channel with real production value and identity can stand out.

## What the winners do well (steal these)
- **Rise-and-fall arc is the product:** setup → peak/hubris → the one fatal mistake → collapse → lesson. Failure stories out-engage success stories.
- **Hook/title formula:** named subject + a number + a loss/stakes word ("How X Lost $8 Billion"). First 2-3s decide the scroll.
- **Real, directed human narration** = their credibility moat vs AI channels.
- **Sourced specific detail** (real numbers/dates) = perceived originality / not mass-produced.
- **Consistent series identity:** one voice, fixed structure, recurring title syntax, title cards.
- **Pacing:** new visual every ~3-5s; bold highlighted karaoke captions.

## Their weaknesses (exploit these)
1. AI-voice channels have weak scripts + flat pacing.
2. **Almost nobody renders original, cited on-screen data** → Aurelis's data cards are a real differentiator.
3. Generic reused stock visuals → everything looks interchangeable.
4. Robotic undirected AI voice, no emotional arc.
5. No series identity (the automation mass is forgettable).
6. Incumbents are long-form + slow; MagnatesMedia dormant → gap for a Shorts-native rise-and-fall channel.
7. Archival-clip-heavy channels are copyright-fragile; Aurelis's stock+AI-still hybrid is structurally safer.

## RETENTION MECHANICS — what competitors + 2026 data say (added Sep 17 2026)
_Prompted by Aurelis's real numbers: 40.1% stayed-to-watch, 92.9% cold Shorts-feed traffic. Goal ~70%._

**Benchmarks (2026):** intro retention (past first 3s) should be **>70%**; strong Shorts = **70-85%**, exceptional >85%. **50-60% of all drop-off happens in the first 3 SECONDS.** Shorts with hooks under 2s have ~30% higher AVD than longer intros. → Matches Aurelis exactly: the swipe-away is at 0-2s, not the body.

**The multimodal hook (all 3 must fire in <2s):**
1. **Visual pattern-interrupt on FRAME 0** — a striking/unexpected first frame that breaks "swipe autopilot." Kill establishing wide shots, logo/intro cards, and dark/muddy openers. Aurelis fix already logged: LEAD every beat-0 with BRIGHT real `vid:` footage; frame-0 hook text hard-on (no fade).
2. **On-screen text promise** — the big serif hook line = the value/curiosity gap, readable silent (muted autoplay). ✅ Aurelis has this; the Sep-17 rewrite made it 2-line peak→collapse + number.
3. **Spoken keyword-rich first sentence** — no "In 20XX…" / "hey guys" throat-clearing; open mid-stakes. ✅ baked into the hook standard.

**Steal these specific competitor tactics:**
- **Open loop / curiosity gap:** state the shocking OUTCOME first, withhold the WHY. ("This $70B company vanished in weeks — and the auditors let it happen.") The unanswered question is what holds them. Company Man / MagnatesMedia lead with the collapse, then rewind to explain.
- **Seamless LOOP (the biggest re-watch lever):** two kinds — (a) **visual loop**: last frame ≈ first frame so the restart is invisible; (b) **narrative loop**: the closing line makes you want to hear the opening line again (ending recontextualizes the hook). Best Shorts do BOTH → viewers rewatch → AVD can exceed 100%. **Aurelis STATUS: partial** — has a 1.3s visual loop-back tail (zoom on opening frame) + audio-loop scripting (last beat rephrases hook). GAP: make the tail frame *identical* to frame 0 (not just "a" opening frame) and make the final spoken line a true call-back to the hook line.
- **Micro-payoffs every few seconds** + a visual change every ~1.5-2.5s so there's never a dead zone (main drop-off is 5-10s — pace UP there). ✅ Aurelis dense-cut engine already does this.
- **Retention benchmark loop:** competitors A/B 3 hook variants on the same short and keep the winner. Aurelis can do this cheaply (Kokoro VO is free) — build 2-3 hook variants, ship, compare 3s-retention in Studio.

**Aurelis-specific gaps to close (priority order):**
