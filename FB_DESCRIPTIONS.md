## #43 Richard Whitney (Aurelis) — https://youtu.be/olt8XOXJYms (YT public)
Wall Street's "White Knight"… ended up in Sing Sing. 😳

On Black Thursday, 1929, Richard Whitney walked onto the Stock Exchange floor and bid $205 a share for U.S. Steel, above the market, to calm the panic. It worked for one day. Five days later the market collapsed anyway. He became president of the Exchange, but his own investments were losing. So he took money from the Exchange's gratuity fund, the New York Yacht Club and his father-in-law's estate. In 1938 he pleaded guilty: five to ten years in Sing Sing.
